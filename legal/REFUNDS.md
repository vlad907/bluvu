# Bluvu Refund Policy

> **DRAFT for review by a lawyer. Not in force.** Placeholders are in [brackets].

- **Try first.** Every media server can run a 14-day trial with all Venue features before you buy.
- **First purchase: 30 days.** If Bluvu isn't right for you, ask within 30 days of your first license
  purchase for a full refund. The license is then revoked.
- **Renewals: 14 days.** A yearly renewal can be refunded in full if you ask within 14 days of the charge
  and haven't used the renewed term (no updates installed, no changes after the renewal date).
- **After that,** fees for the current term aren't refunded, except under the warranty in the EULA
  (section 7.1: a pro-rata refund for the unused part of the term).
- **Upgrades** can be reversed within 14 days; you're refunded the prorated charge.
- **Hardware** is covered by its own warranty and return terms on the quote.
- **How:** email [email address] with your license key, or use the customer portal. Refunds go back to the
  original payment method through Paddle, usually within 10 business days.
