## What we collect

| From | What | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Buying a license | Name, business name, email, billing country, what you bought. Card details go to **Paddle** (our merchant of record), never to us. | To issue and renew the license, send the key and receipts, and support you. |
| The media server, when it activates or refreshes | The license key; **hashes** of the PC's identifiers (not the identifiers themselves); the Bluvu version; the number of screens in use; the PC's IP address. | To check the license, bind it to that PC, deliver updates, and catch misuse. |
| The website | Server logs (IP address, pages, time) kept [30] days. No advertising or tracking cookies. | To run and secure the site. |
| Contact and quote forms | What you send us. | To answer you. |

**What we never collect:** your layouts, video, pictures, media files, stream names, or anything shown on
your screens. Receiving PCs never contact us.
