Mix and match

Resolutions mix freely on one server. The inside bar runs one 4K stream and ten 1080p screens at 30 fps from one server. Screens on the server's own ports are on top of the network ones.

Network sizing

Plan on about 70% of a link: 1 GbE carries about 7 screens at 1080p30, 2.5 GbE about 17, 10 GbE about 70. Receivers only take in their own screens, so 1 GbE is plenty for most.

Proven on the GPU

The Bluvu engine has rendered 4 × 4K and 24 × 1080p at a steady 60 fps with no dropped frames. Server ratings leave headroom for NDI encoding, video decoding and sound.

Planning figures. Actual capacity depends on the sources, the network and the layout; every Bluvu system is burned in on your own layout before it ships, and we'll size it with you first.
